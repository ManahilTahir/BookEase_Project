About This Diagram

The Level 0 DFD (context diagram) defines the boundary of the BookEase system as a single unified process (Process 0) interacting with all external entities and the persistent database.

  • Customer — External client who browses services, specialists, and books appointments via the Responsive Web Portal and mobile PWA.
  • Staff Specialist — Internal practitioner who manages appointment statuses, calendar schedules, and client histories via the Desktop Application.
  • Business Owner — Management executive who configures working schedules, services, pricing, business settings, and reviews financial KPI reports.
  • System Administrator — IT administrator with elevated privileges who manages user accounts, role permissions, system configuration, backups, and audit logs.
  • SQLite Database (WAL Mode) — Shared transactional storage ensuring concurrent reads, atomic booking locks, and zero data collisions between web and desktop users.
